## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Identity
This design system honors Indonesian traditional culinary heritage (*jajanan pasar*) through the lens of modern, hygienic, and refined craftsmanship. It balances the nostalgic warmth of early-morning market aromas (*aroma daun pisang, kelapa parut, dan gula aren*) with the crisp efficiency of contemporary gourmet e-commerce.

The visual language rejects cluttered, overly kitschy folklore tropes. Instead, it embodies **Artisanal Minimalist Gastronomy**:
- **Warm & Authentic:** Evoking tactile organic textures, steaming bamboo baskets, and hand-wrapped banana leaf parcels.
- **Hygienic & Premium:** Clear surface hierarchy, clean whitespace, and pristine culinary framing that assures high food safety standards.
- **Culturally Grounded yet Modern:** Expressive editorial serif typography paired with structured, highly legible micro-copy and utilitarian data tables.

### Emotional Response
The interface should immediately awaken appetite, evoke comforting memories of home and ritual gatherings (*arisan, slametan, hantaran*), and project trust and punctual delivery reliability for corporate and family catering orders.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from native culinary elements: crisp pandan leaves, caramelized coconut palm sugar (*gula aren*), unpolished rice husk (*kulit beras*), and warm charcoal smoke.

### Core Roles & Tokens
- **Primary (`#2D6A4F` - Pandan Deep):** The core brand anchor. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key banners, and organic freshness signifiers. A deeper shade (`#1B4332`) is reserved for hover states and high-contrast badges.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Gula Merah Warm Gold):** The appetite trigger and promotional accent. Applied to ratings, specialty badges (*Kue Tampah*, *Snack Box Custom*), notifications, and highlighted price points.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309` - Terracotta Bakar):** Grounding artisanal accent used for editorial tags, artisanal callouts, hover states of secondary actions, and seasonal stamps.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1F2421` - Arang Bakar):** Soft charcoal with warm undertones, ensuring high legibility while avoiding the harshness of pure black. Used for headers, primary labels, and dense data text.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FDFBF7` - Tepung Beras / Krem Alami):** The default background tone, replacing sterile digital white with an organic, linen-soft parchment feel.
- **Surface Muted / Well (`#F4EFE6` - Bambu Kering):** Used for cards, tables, input fills, and separated panel backgrounds.
- **Border / Hairline (`#E6DEC9`):** Low-contrast organic divider color providing subtle boundary definition without clutter.

### Semantic Tones
- **Freshness Badge / Info:** `#2D6A4F` (Text: `#FFFFFF`, Tint Surface: `#E8F5E9`)
- **Warning / Pre-order Alert:** `#D97706` (Text: `#FFFFFF`, Tint Surface: `#FEF3C7`)
- **Error / Stock Out:** `#B91C1C` (Text: `#FFFFFF`, Tint Surface: `#FEE2E2`)

## Typography

The typographical pairing strikes an intentional duality between culture and commerce:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys editorial elegance, heirloom pride, and high gastronomic status. Used exclusively for brand banners, collection hero titles, culinary narrative highlights, and product detail names.
- **Functional Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A modern, warm geometric grotesque designed natively with Indonesian clarity in mind. It excels at small sizes, product metadata, ingredient lists, prices, order trackers, and dense admin tables.

### Rules of Application
1. **Never use `Playfair Display` in dense functional UI:** Forms, table headers, breadcrumbs, buttons, and numeric metrics must always use `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
2. **Numeric Styling:** Prices (e.g., `Rp 45.000`) and SKU codes must be set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with `font-weight: 600` or `700` and tabular numeric lining where available to preserve vertical grid alignment.
3. **Caps Usage:** All-caps styling is reserved strictly for `label-sm` (e.g., badges such as `DIBUAT PAGI INI`, `HABIS`, `PRE-ORDER`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of `1280px` for catalog views and `780px` for storytelling/blog content.

### Grid & Breakpoint Rhythm
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter-desktop` (`1.5rem` / 24px), page margin `margin-desktop` (`4rem` / 64px). Catalog grid displays 4 items per row; tampah bundle customizer splits 8 cols (builder canvas) / 4 cols (live tray summary).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter-desktop` (`1.5rem` / 24px), page margin `margin-tablet` (`2rem` / 32px). Catalog grid reflows to 3 or 2 items per row.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (`1rem` / 16px), page margin `margin-mobile` (`1rem` / 16px). Product listing defaults to a structured 2-column card layout with compact vertical padding.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section spacing follows proportional multiples of 16px:
- In-card elements: `space-xs` (8px) to `space-md` (16px).
- Between card groups / admin table rows: `space-lg` (24px).
- Between major storefront sections: `space-2xl` (48px) on mobile, scaling to `space-3xl` (72px) on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the tactile, artisanal character while upholding a clean digital standard, the design system avoids heavy, artificial grey shadows. Visual hierarchy is established through **Tonal Layering** paired with **Organic Ambient Shadows** tinted with warm brown undertones.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#FDFBF7`. Completely flat, non-elevated surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Card & Well Base):** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFE6` resting on canvas with a crisp organic outline: `1px solid #E6DEC9`. No blur is applied in rest state.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Dropdowns):** Subtle lift using an organic umber-tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 36, 27, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 36, 27, 0.04);`
  Retains the `1px solid #E6DEC9` hairline border.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Tampah Cart Drawer & Quickview):** Higher focal elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.16), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08);`
  Backed by a warm frosted backdrop filter (`rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

### Elevation Rules
- Never use pure black (`#000000`) for shadows; always tint shadows with warm organic undertones (`rgba(45, 36, 27, x)`).
- Highlight elevated states through subtle border color transitions: transitioning from `#E6DEC9` to primary `#2D6A4F` on hover/focus.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry, reflecting the organic, handcrafted nature of molded traditional cakes (such as *kue ku*, *lumpur*, and *lemper*) while maintaining a disciplined architectural baseline.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form fields, inputs, action buttons, table containers, and snack box slot placeholders.
- **Cards & Banners (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Catalog product cards, tampah configurator panels, customer review cards, and blog teaser tiles.
- **Hero & Dialogs (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Primary landing banners, modal dialogs, and seasonal promo takeovers.
- **Badges & Quantities (`rounded-full` / Pill):** Freshness pills, price tags, category filter chips, and circular quantity counters (`+` / `-`).
